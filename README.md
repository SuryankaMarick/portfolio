### 📋 Professional Summary

My name is Suryanka Marick, and I am a Data Science graduate student, full-stack software developer, and freelance visual designer based in Budapest, Hungary. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering from Chitkara University in India, complemented by an international Computer Science exchange program at Providence University in Taiwan. During my exchange in Taiwan, my conversational language research directly inspired me to build and deploy Streamit, a live language-learning web application. Professionally, I worked as a Data Science Consulting Intern at MathCo in Bangalore, where I engineered a multivariate regression and discount optimization pipeline for MARS Inc. processing approximately 7.8 million data points across 640+ products and two years of transactional records. By modeling pricing elasticity and promotional timing, my solution improved discount decision accuracy by 15 percent over heuristic baselines and powered a deployed decision-support dashboard. Alongside my industry consulting work, I developed a real-time American Sign Language recognition system using MediaPipe and a 4-layer CNN that achieved 94.2 percent accuracy while reducing prediction flicker by 78 percent on standard CPU hardware. My technical foundation is further backed by credentials including the IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate, Oracle GenAI and Data Science Professional Certificate, Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900), and a Deloitte Data Analytics simulation. Currently, I am a Stipendium Hungaricum Scholar at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest, completing my preparatory coursework toward my Master of Science in Data Science. Alongside my graduate studies in mathematics, programming, and analysis, I am actively learning the Hungarian language to integrate into the local academic and professional community. Looking ahead, I plan to specialize in deep network development, stream mining, and scalable optimization during my main MSc research phase at ELTE. After graduating, I aim to join an international technology or R&D organization as a Machine Learning Engineer or Applied Data Scientist. Ultimately, my goal is to bridge statistical modeling, full-stack engineering, and visual design to build reliable, accessible AI products that create measurable global impact.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. 🌐 Streamit — Real-Time Global Language Exchange Platform (`2025`)
* **Live Deployment:** [streamit-learning-app.onrender.com](https://streamit-learning-app.onrender.com)
* **Overview:** A full-stack real-time communication and language-learning platform inspired by conversational language research conducted during my academic exchange in Taiwan.
* **Tech Stack:** `MongoDB` `Express.js` `React.js` `Node.js` `TypeScript` `WebRTC` `Stream.io` `Tailwind CSS`
* **Highlights:** Peer-to-peer low-latency video calling, live screen sharing, instant messaging, and learner matching.

### 2. 📊 MARS Inc. Discount Optimizer & Analytics Dashboard (`2026`)
* **Overview:** End-to-end econometric modeling and pricing optimization system built during my Data Science Consulting internship at MathCo.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python` `Scikit-Learn` `Pandas` `NumPy` `SQL` `Statistical Modeling` `BI Dashboarding`
* **Highlights:** Processed ~7.8M regional data points across 640+ ASINs, delivered optimal ASIN-level discount thresholds, and boosted decision accuracy by 15%.

### 3. 🤟 Real-Time ASL Hand Gesture Recognition (`CNN + Temporal Stabilization`)
* **Overview:** Low-latency computer vision pipeline translating American Sign Language gestures into text on consumer CPU hardware.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python` `TensorFlow / Keras` `MediaPipe` `OpenCV` `NumPy`
* **Highlights:**
  * Trained a 4-layer CNN (1.2M parameters) achieving **94.2% accuracy** across 29 ASL classes on a 490K-sample dataset.
  * Deployed confidence-aware sliding-window aggregation ($N=15$, 680ms buffer), cutting prediction flicker from **32% to 7% (78% improvement)** with zero retraining.
  * Runs smoothly at **22 FPS** on CPU-only hardware (Intel Core i5, 8 GB RAM).
